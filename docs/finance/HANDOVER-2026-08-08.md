## The salary voucher — what was unknown, and what the data said

Two questions blocked this for weeks. Both are now answered from the June-2026 run
`939efccb-e7ea-4194-b059-db9b9c200a7d`, not from assumption.

### The extra MAS columns are C-suite remuneration

`Amount = col4 + col5` on every row of the MAS voucher; the IDC voucher has no such columns.
They are one figure cut in two. At each branch a **single employee** reproduces all four of
col4's headline figures at once:

| Branch | gross / payable / PF-er / TDS | Who |
|---|---|---|
| HEAD OFFICE | 166,262 / 133,462 / 16,800 / 16,000 | `MAS00001` — Chief Executive Officer |
| AHMEDABAD-JALDARSHAN | 99,598 / 84,958 / 8,640 / 5,800 | `MAS02477` — Chief Operations Officer |
| NOIDA, NOIDA-2 | all zero | neither branch has a `CHIEF%` employee |

The two **zero** branches are what make this conclusive rather than coincidental.

Two earlier hypotheses are dead and recorded so nobody retries them: not trainees (Head Office
has 16 ONROLL and 0 trainees yet col4 is 166,262), and not offroll (both are `ONROLL`).

### IDC cannot be generated from this database

`employee_code` carries the entity. But **mas_hrms holds zero IDC-coded employees** out of
58,627, and `NOIDA-DIALDESK` — an IDC voucher branch — has 149 employees and **0 active**. No
code produces a voucher for a population the database does not contain.

### What the generator does

Reproduces the June-2026 HEAD OFFICE voucher **exactly — 10 of 10 ledger lines** — running the
real service against real payroll.

`Gross Salary` is the **balancing plug**, not payroll gross: all credits minus the three
employer debits. Using payroll gross leaves the voucher out by 1,167 at Head Office, and Tally
rejects an unbalanced journal on import. Payroll gross is reported alongside as `payroll_gross`
so the gap stays visible.

Other branches differ because the HRMS2 run holds **more employees** than the legacy voucher's
population — every delta is positive and uniform. Head Office is where the two populations
agree, and there it is perfect. That is a data-vintage difference, not an arithmetic one.

## Addendum, 2026-08-08 — the Imprest Details format

The supplied `Imprest_Details` workbook is now the imprest report's format, exactly:

```
S.No. | Date | GRN | Exp. Head | Exp. SubHead | INFLOW | OUTFLOW | Balance |
Mode | Chq No | Bank | Remarks
```

`Balance = previous + INFLOW − OUTFLOW` holds on every row of the reference, and its own numbers
are the test fixture — 25 rows from an implied opening of 19,817.96 down to 7,675.26, with
12,142.70 of outflow and no inflow. The total row puts **"Total" in the Exp. SubHead column**
and leaves **Balance blank**, because the total of a running balance is meaningless.

This REPLACED the generic ledger CSV added earlier the same day. That one produced a
non-conforming file, and keeping both would have left a wrong export sitting next to the right
one for somebody to pick.

Also fixed on the way: `src/hooks/useInactivityTimeout.ts` used `NodeJS.Timeout`, a namespace
that does not exist in the app's tsconfig, so it failed any scoped typecheck that reached it.
Now `ReturnType<typeof setTimeout>`.
