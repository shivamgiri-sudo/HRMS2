The voucher chain — raise, approve, release

Per payment
Finance Head
Raise
Picks the bank account and the ledger head, enters the amount and remarks. Nothing has moved yet — this is a request, not a payment.
CEO
Approve
A plain yes/no on amount and purpose. Does not execute the transfer — only clears it to be released.
Accounts Head
Release
The only step that actually moves money: keys in the real payment mode, date and transaction reference, then posts the Bank Ledger row and (for vendor payments) the TDS-withheld entry.

Why three different people

A role can be faked by a shared login; a person can't be two people at once. So the rule is enforced on identity, not title:

raised_by ≠ ceo_approved_by ≠ released_by

Even a super_admin account cannot self-approve or self-release its own voucher — the check runs server-side on every release.

3

Bank Reconciliation — matching HRMS against the real bank

Monthly, by Accounts Head
Step 1
Start period & upload statement
Opening balance carries forward from the last closed period. Upload any bank CSV/XLSX; map its columns once (date, description, debit/credit) and every later upload from that bank reuses the mapping.
Step 2
Auto-match, then resolve the rest
Exact amount within a ±15-day window auto-matches. Anything left over is matched by hand, or posted as an Adjustment — a real bank-only entry like a bank charge or interest, which lands as a new Bank Ledger row.
Step 3
Close the period
Only allowed once every statement line is resolved and the numbers actually balance (see the formula below). Closing locks every matched entry and carries the closing balance into the next period.

What "close" actually checks

Not a rubber stamp — the classic bank-reconciliation formula, enforced server-side before a period can lock:

HRMS closing balance − outstanding (issued, not yet cleared) = statement closing balance

A mismatch refuses the close and shows the exact rupee difference — never forced through, and never silently accepted.

4

What comes out the other end

Tally export

Provisional

Any range that touches an open (not-yet-closed) period. Watermarked in the exported XML and flagged in the UI — a draft hand-off, not the final posting. This is the state every export was in before Bank Reconciliation existed.

Final

Every entry in the requested range belongs to a closed reconciliation period. Only then does the export drop its provisional watermark — safe to post into Tally as the real record.
